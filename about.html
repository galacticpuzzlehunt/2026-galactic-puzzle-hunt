About

  • The puzzlehunt starts on Friday, October 9, 2026 at 6:28 PM EDT and ends on Monday, October 19, 2026 at 6:28 PM EDT.
  • At that time, solutions will be posted and hints will be closed, but teams can continue to register and submit answers until Thursday, November 19, 2026 at 6:28 PM EST. After this date the site will be archived (puzzles will still be solvable but team progress will not be tracked).
  • Teams have a maximum size of 8, though we believe a size of 5 to 6 will offer the best experience. If you would like to include additional inexperienced puzzlers on your team, please email us and we’ll consider your request.
  • Changed this year: The teams page won’t list the exact number of solved puzzles. It will still roughly show your progress through the hunt. We’re making this change to encourage teams to experience the hunt at their own pace.
  • You will have 20 total guesses for each puzzle. If you legitimately run out of guesses and need more, feel free to contact us.
  • Starting on the first day and continuing throughout the hunt, teams will receive hints that they can use on puzzles. These can be used to ask a question (e.g. “which answers to these clues are wrong?”) or suggest the next step (e.g. “use Morse code”).
  • If you have any questions about the hunt, or if you want to contact us for any reason, email contact@galacticpuzzlehunt.com. We will get back to you as soon as we can.
  • No puzzles in the hunt have the answer FAIR PLAY.

What are the puzzles like?

We expect this puzzlehunt to be comparable in length to previous Galactic Puzzle Hunts or Teammate Hunt (that is, on the harder and longer side for online puzzlehunts). Be aware that finishing a puzzlehunt of this size is a major achievement!

If you are new to puzzlehunts and are interested in seeing some examples, we recommend looking at the above hunts, Puzzle Potluck, Silph Puzzlehunt, or Paradox Puzzlehunt. In a puzzlehunt, each puzzle has an underlying pattern or insight, which you need to figure out (somewhat like the theme of a crossword puzzle). Puzzles can come in many different forms; the only real commonality is that you usually receive no direct instructions, so it’s up to you to figure out how to make sense of the information you’re given. You can read a longer introduction to puzzlehunts here.

What is and isn’t allowed?

  • You may not view source code, dump network packets, scrape, hack, or otherwise interact with the website in any unintended way. If you’re not sure whether something is intended, email contact@galacticpuzzlehunt.com.
  • You may not ask an LLM or other generative AI to solve a puzzle or a significant part of one. Treat this like our previous rule about not looking at source code: it bypasses our intended solving experience and goes against the spirit of the hunt. More limited uses, such as doing research into a topic you’re unfamiliar with, or interpreting an individual crossword clue you’re stuck on, are left to your discretion. We urge you to treat LLMs as tools and to use them only to the extent that they make the hunt more enjoyable for you.
  • You may use any other external sources for help, including other people, as long as they aren’t helping other teams and aren’t actively participating in the hunt.
  • You may not stream or post spoilers for the hunt in a place where other teams might see them.
  • You may not create multiple accounts or be on multiple teams.
  • We reserve the right to disqualify any team for unsportsmanlike conduct.
